Why Aurelia Grove

A Thoughtful Approach to Every Detail.

Building a custom home is a long collaboration. These are the principles this concept brand is built around — the way we'd want to be treated if it were our home.

  1. 01

    Personalized Planning

    We start with how you live — mornings, guests, work, storage, the view you want from the kitchen sink — and let the plan grow from there.

  2. 02

    Design-Led Thinking

    Proportion, daylight and materials are considered early, alongside cost, so good design is part of the budget rather than an add-on.

  3. 03

    Clear Communication

    Regular updates, written decisions and a single point of contact. You should always know what's happening, what's next and what it costs.

  4. 04

    Quality-Focused Approach

    Careful trade coordination, inspections at critical stages and attention to the details you'll touch every day.

Common Questions

Straight answers to the questions people ask before building a custom home. If yours isn't here, just ask.

There isn't an honest single number. Cost depends on size, design complexity, site conditions, finishes, utilities and municipal fees. Rather than quoting a generic price per square foot, we build a budget framework from your goals and lot during planning, then refine it as the design develops — so you see how decisions affect cost before you commit to them.

No. Many people reach out while they're still searching. We can talk through what to look for in a lot — zoning, utilities, slope, orientation and setbacks — and, if you're considering a specific parcel, help you understand its potential before you buy.

It varies widely. Design and permitting can take several months on their own, and construction duration depends on the size and complexity of the home, the site and the availability of materials and inspections. We'll give you a realistic schedule for your specific project once we understand its scope.

We coordinate the permit process with the designers and engineers on your project, prepare submissions and respond to plan-check comments. Requirements vary by city and county, and some lots need extra reviews — for example HOA approval, design review or fire-zone requirements — which we identify early.

Yes. We can collaborate with your architect, offering constructability and cost feedback during design. If you don't have one, our design-build approach coordinates the design team for you.

By pricing early and often. Cost checkpoints happen during design, allowances are documented in writing and any change is priced before work proceeds. No builder can promise that nothing will change, but clear documentation means you're making informed decisions rather than discovering surprises.

Nothing formal. Helpful extras include a lot address (if you have one), photos or links to homes you love, a rough sense of size and rooms, and a budget range you're comfortable discussing. If you're unsure about any of it, that's exactly what the first conversation is for.
