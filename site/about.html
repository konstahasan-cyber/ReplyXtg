About Aurelia Grove

Homes Shaped by the People Who Live in Them.

Aurelia Grove is a concept design-build studio for custom homes in Sacramento and its surrounding communities.

Our point of view

Good Design Is a Way of Paying Attention.

We believe a home should feel effortless to live in — and that feeling comes from hundreds of small, considered decisions: the width of a hallway, the height of a window sill, the place you drop your keys.

So we start with conversation, not catalogs. We study your lot and your routines, then shape light, space and materials around them. Throughout, we keep cost and schedule visible, because a beautiful plan is only valuable if it can be built.

Why Aurelia Grove

A Thoughtful Approach to Every Detail.

Building a custom home is a long collaboration. These are the principles this concept brand is built around — the way we'd want to be treated if it were our home.

  1. 01

    Personalized Planning

    We start with how you live — mornings, guests, work, storage, the view you want from the kitchen sink — and let the plan grow from there.

  2. 02

    Design-Led Thinking

    Proportion, daylight and materials are considered early, alongside cost, so good design is part of the budget rather than an add-on.

  3. 03

    Clear Communication

    Regular updates, written decisions and a single point of contact. You should always know what's happening, what's next and what it costs.

  4. 04

    Quality-Focused Approach

    Careful trade coordination, inspections at critical stages and attention to the details you'll touch every day.

Our commitments

What Working Together Should Feel Like.

Promises about outcomes are easy to make. These are commitments about how we work.

  • 01

    We'll talk about money early

    Budget isn't an awkward topic saved for later. It's a design input from the first conversation.

  • 02

    We'll put decisions in writing

    Selections, allowances and changes are documented, so nothing depends on memory.

  • 03

    We'll tell you what we don't know yet

    Some answers — like permit timelines or soil conditions — need research. We'll say so, and explain how we'll find out.

  • 04

    We'll respect your time

    Organized meetings, clear agendas and decisions sequenced so you're never overwhelmed.
